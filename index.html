Jarvis Home

A private assistant for one household.

Jarvis Home is a voice assistant that one family runs on a Mac in its own home. It answers questions in the house's rooms and in the Jarvis Home app on each family member's phone.

Each family member can choose to connect a few of their own accounts, such as their email, calendar or files, so they can ask Jarvis about them. Those connections belong to that person, stay encrypted on the family's Mac, and are never shared with anyone else in the house.

Jarvis Home is not a commercial product and is not available to the public.

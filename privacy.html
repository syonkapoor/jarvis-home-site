Jarvis HomePrivacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Jarvis Home is a private assistant that one household runs on a Mac in its own home, for the people who live there. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to anyone outside that household. This policy explains what happens to your information when you connect one of your own accounts to it.

What Jarvis Home can reach

Nothing is connected until you connect it yourself from the Jarvis Home app on your phone. When you connect a Google account, you allow Jarvis Home to:

  • search and read your Gmail. Only when you approve each action, it can also send an email, archive messages, mark them read or unread, add or remove one of your existing labels, or move messages to Trash, where Gmail keeps them for 30 days and you can restore them. It never deletes an email permanently and never creates a label;
  • read your Google Calendar, and add events only when you approve each one;
  • search and read files in your Google Drive, and create a new Google Doc or Sheet only when you approve it. It cannot edit, rename, move, share or delete any file, including the ones it created.

Other services you connect (for example Outlook, WHOOP, Canvas or another email account) work the same way: Jarvis Home can reach only what you approved when you connected them. Each person can connect at most five services.

How it is used

  • Your accounts are read only when you ask Jarvis something that needs them, and only to answer you. Nothing reads them in the background.
  • Sending a message, tidying your inbox, creating an event or a file, or any other change happens only after you approve it on your own phone, behind Face ID. The approval shows exactly what will change before anything does.
  • To work out an answer, your request and the relevant content (for example the text of an email) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on this data. Content is not sent to any other AI provider.
  • Your information is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile of you.

Jarvis Home's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Where it is kept

  • The sign-in for each account you connect is stored encrypted on the household's Mac. It is not stored on any other server.
  • Your conversations with Jarvis about your accounts are kept in a private log for you on that Mac, separate from the household's shared history. The oldest entries are dropped as it grows.
  • Answers about your accounts are not saved in the household's shared transcripts or its voice cache.
  • The Mac and your phone talk over an encrypted connection that only your paired phone can use.

Who can see it

  • Answers you ask for in the app go to your own phone only.
  • If you ask about your accounts at a speaker in a room, Jarvis first checks that it is you (by your voice, your face on a home camera, or a passcode you set) and then speaks the answer in that room, where anyone nearby can hear it.
  • The household member who looks after the Mac has administrator access to it and so could technically reach what is stored there.

Removing access and deleting your data

  • Remove a connection in the Jarvis Home app at any time. Its stored sign-in is deleted from the Mac immediately.
  • You can also revoke access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or the matching page of any other service.
  • Ask the household member who runs Jarvis Home to delete everything about you (your connections, your private log and your settings), and it will be removed from the Mac.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen, or to the household member who runs Jarvis Home.

Jarvis Home is run privately by one household for its own members.
